## 📌 1. Project Overview

The **ApnoMart Sales Performance Dashboard** is a Business Intelligence project developed using Microsoft Power BI to analyse sales data and generate meaningful business insights.

The main objective of this project is to transform raw sales data into interactive visualizations that help business managers understand sales performance, profitability, customer behaviour, product performance and regional distribution.

This dashboard provides an executive-level overview of business performance and supports data-driven decision-making.

---

## 🎯 2. Project Objectives

- Analyse overall sales and profit performance.
- Identify the highest revenue-generating product categories.
- Monitor monthly sales trends.
- Compare profitability across different regions.
- Understand customer segmentation and purchasing behaviour.
- Analyse payment method preferences.
- Identify top-performing products.
- Generate strategic recommendations for business improvement.

## 🔍 7. Key Business Insights

### Insight 1: Revenue Pattern

Furniture is the highest revenue-generating category, contributing approximately **2.91M** in sales, followed by Office Supplies at **2.81M**. Electronics records the lowest sales among the five categories at approximately **2.32M**.

**Business Impact:** Furniture and Office Supplies are important revenue contributors. The company should maintain sufficient inventory and focus on effective marketing strategies for these categories.

### Insight 2: Profitability Pattern

The West region generates the highest profit at approximately **0.80M**, followed by Central at **0.77M** and East at **0.72M**. North records the lowest regional profit at approximately **0.64M**.

**Business Impact:** The company should investigate the lower profitability of the North region and compare its discount strategies, product mix and operating costs with stronger-performing regions.

### Insight 3: Customer Behaviour Pattern

Small Business customers contribute the largest sales share at **26.63%**, followed by Consumer at **25.45%**, Corporate at **25.14%** and Home Office at **22.77%**.

Cash is the most popular payment method in the displayed dashboard, contributing approximately **22.4%**.

**Business Impact:** ApnoMart has a relatively balanced customer base. The company should maintain services for all customer segments and provide multiple payment options.

*Note: These figures reflect the date-filter selection displayed in the dashboard screenshot.*

## 💡 8. Strategic Business Recommendation

ApnoMart should focus on maintaining strong sales in Furniture and Office Supplies while improving profitability in the North region.

Recommended actions:

1. Maintain sufficient inventory of high-performing product categories.
2. Review discount strategies and operating costs in the North region.
3. Study the successful performance of the West region.
4. Improve customer engagement through targeted marketing campaigns.
5. Monitor sales, profit and profit margins regularly.

These strategies can help improve business profitability and support sustainable growth.
